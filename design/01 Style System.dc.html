Iconography

{{ i.px }} · {{ i.use }}
44 touch target
  • One outline set, 1.5px stroke at 20px, round caps and joins. Scales to 1.5px at 16 and 24 (stroke not scaled).
  • Filled variant only to signal a selected/active state (wishlist saved, filled star). Never mix a second icon family.
  • Icons inherit currentColor. Optically centered on a 20px box; inline icons align to text cap height.
  • Every icon-only control has a visible label on focus or an aria-label, and ≥ 44px hit area on touch.

Image system

{{ im.label }}
{{ im.name }} · {{ im.rule }}
  • Product media default: 4:5 frame, object-fit contain on Surface Secondary so cut-outs and studio shots never crop. Merchant may switch to cover.
  • “Adapt” ratio takes the first image’s natural ratio for the whole grid row group.
  • Fallback: Surface Secondary block with a 1.5px outline placeholder glyph, or the title centered for collection cards. Never a broken image, never an illustration.
  • Loading: Surface Secondary skeleton at final aspect ratio; fade-in Standard on decode. No layout shift.
